TechGadgets Return Policy

At TechGadgets, customer satisfaction is our priority. We offer a comprehensive return policy to ensure you're happy with your purchase.

Return Window

Standard items: 30 days from delivery date
Electronics: 14 days from delivery date
Opened software: Non-returnable due to licensing restrictions
Eligible Returns
Items must be in original condition with all packaging, accessories, and documentation. We accept returns for:

Defective or damaged products
Wrong item received
Changed mind (subject to restocking fee of 15%)
Refund Process

Refunds processed within 5-7 business days after receiving returned item
Original payment method will be credited
Shipping costs are non-refundable unless item was defective
How to Initiate a Return

Log into your account at www.techgadgets.com
Go to "Order History" and select the item
Click "Request Return" and select reason
Print the prepaid return label
Package item securely and drop off at any authorized location
Non-Returnable Items

Gift cards
Downloadable software
Personal care items
Items marked as "Final Sale"
For questions about returns, contact our support team at returns@techgadgets.com or call 1-800-TECH-HELP.
