Delivery Policy
TechGadgets Delivery Policy

We strive to get your orders to you quickly and safely.

Shipping Options

Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days (FREE on orders over $50)
Express Shipping: 2-3 business days ($12.99)
Overnight Shipping: Next business day ($24.99)
Delivery Timeframes
Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same business day. Weekend orders ship Monday. Delivery times exclude weekends and holidays.

Shipping Locations
We ship to all 50 US states, including PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses. International shipping available to Canada and Mexico (additional fees apply).

Order Tracking
You'll receive a tracking number via email within 24 hours of shipment. Track your package at www.techgadgets.com/track or through the carrier's website.

Delivery Issues

Missing Package: Contact us within 48 hours of expected delivery. We'll investigate with the carrier and provide a resolution.
Damaged in Transit: Refuse the package or take photos of damage before opening. Contact us immediately at shipping@techgadgets.com.
Wrong Address: Address changes can be made up to 2 hours after order placement. Contact customer service immediately.
Signature Requirements
Orders over $500 require adult signature at delivery. Someone 18+ must be present to sign.

Delivery Restrictions
We cannot deliver to:

Private mailbox services (we ship to physical addresses only)
Freight forwarding addresses
Locations outside our shipping zones
For delivery questions, email shipping@techgadgets.com or call 1-800-TECH-HELP.
